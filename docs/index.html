What it shows

The queue

What the estate is working on and what is waiting, read from the state rather than remembered.

The kit graph

Which kits exist, which repos load them, and which calls a kit does not answer.

The fleet

Every repository, its state and its recent runs — with an export, so you can take the numbers somewhere else.

Your language

The whole console in English, हिन्दी or ગુજરાતી, and a theme toggle that stays put.

Why offline is the point

A console for your own machinery should not need someone else's server to open. Nothing on this page loads from the network either: no analytics, no fonts, no badges fetched from anywhere.
